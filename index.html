Note No. YX‑2026‑001 Student Voice Note · Not Legal Tender

Section One

The proposals

Four problems worth solving, and how I would go about proposing solutions to them. None of this happens without discussion — each card is honest about what still needs approval.

The problem

Students need a clearer way to raise ideas, questions and concerns — and to know whether anything happened afterwards.

The proposal

Set up a student feedback and update system, where students can submit ideas or questions and receive regular responses or updates on where things stand.

How it would work

  1. Listen
  2. Review
  3. Respond
  4. Update

Requires school approval to set up and run.

Where it stands now

Homework Club currently gives students a quiet place and time to complete homework while waiting for parents or siblings.

What I'd propose instead

A redesigned version built around daily student-led learning sessions — a different presenter and topic each day, with presenters chosen through interviews.

  • At least 30 minutes of presentation
  • A quiz afterwards
  • A task to demonstrate understanding
  • House points proposed as an incentive, subject to school approval

How it would work

  1. Present
  2. Quiz
  3. Task
  4. Prove understanding

This is a proposal to redesign Homework Club, not a promise that it will replace it — subject to school approval.

The proposal

Explore the possibility of a permanent school-items booth selling genuinely useful student necessities — stationery, clips and similar items — subject to school and PTA approval.

Where the funds could go

Supporting agreed student or school improvements, potentially including washroom and hygiene improvements.

Transparency

Money in Money out What it funded

Any funding model would need clear, public records covering all three.

This funding system has not been approved. It is a proposal to explore, subject to school and PTA approval.

The question

I want to explore whether the current representation and voting structure can be made broader, and whether younger or newer students should have a more appropriate voice.

Proposed discussion

How can the student voice be represented more broadly? No specific final structure is promised here — that would need to come out of proper discussion.
