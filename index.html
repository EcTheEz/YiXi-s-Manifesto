A closer look

Your idea shouldn't disappear into a box.

The point of the Student Voice Board is a visible loop: what was raised, whether it's been discussed, what's possible, whether it happened — and if it can't, why. Submitting something never guarantees it's accepted; the promise is a more transparent process, not a yes to everything.

Section Four

The 4-step promise

This is how every proposal above is meant to move — and why I can't promise the school will implement all of it. I can promise the process.

01

Listen

Gather student feedback.

02

Propose

Turn recurring problems into concrete proposals.

03

Negotiate

Bring proposals to the appropriate staff or PTA, and work through the constraints.

04

Report

Tell students what happened — including when something wasn't approved.

Transparency means reporting outcomes — not just the successes.
